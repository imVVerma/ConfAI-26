Partner With ConfAI 2026

ConfAI 2026 offers companies and organizations a platform to connect with leading researchers, faculty, students, and industry professionals shaping the future of AI. Hosted by the Harish and Bina Shah School of AI & Computer Science at Plaksha University, sponsoring the event provides access to top talent, research initiatives and strategic industry visibility.

Why Partner with ConfAI 2026

Strategic collaboration opportunities at the forefront of India's AI research and innovation ecosystem.

01

Research Exchange & Product Showcase

Dedicated speaking slots to share research, showcase products, or present applied AI work — a format that fits R&D labs, product teams, and industry practitioners alike.

Contribute to a program spanning tracks across healthcare, agriculture, robotics, security, and foundational AI/ML — shaping India's AI agenda across sectors.

02

Network Expansion

Engage directly with AI researchers, PhD scholars, and postdoctoral fellows from India's premier institutions.

Build lasting research relationships through networking sessions, technical discussions, and open exchange across the conference.

03

AI Ecosystem Presence & Footprint

Align with a selective, research-first AI conference (~4% abstract acceptance) hosted by Plaksha, spanning domains including healthcare, agriculture, robotics, security, and foundational AI/ML.

Establish a considered footprint within India's AI research community across academic and industry audiences.
