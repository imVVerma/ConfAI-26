Conference Overview

The three day academic conference aims to highlight the potential of AI in exploring effective solutions to societal challenges. The talks and discussions aim to foster collaboration, knowledge sharing, and innovative discussions. It will bring together students, researchers, and practitioners across diverse AI domains to shape the future of research, development, and impactful applications in the Indian AI landscape.

Why Attend ConfAI 2026?

  • Prominent Keynote & Guest Speakers

    Engage with distinguished global leaders and visionary thinkers from academia and industry delivering thought-provoking keynote addresses across cutting-edge AI tracks.

  • Latest AI Research

    Discover peer-reviewed breakthroughs, state-of-the-art methodology, and transformative discoveries presented by pioneering researchers pushing the frontier of artificial intelligence.

  • Practical Frameworks & Insights

    Acquire actionable architectures, real-world tools, and strategic insights addressing crucial questions around AI governance, scalable deployment, and societal impact.

  • Collaborative Networking

    Connect with peer researchers, esteemed faculty, and tech innovators through structured networking forums designed to spark multidisciplinary research collaborations.

  • Diverse Presentation Formats

    Experience an engaging blend of dynamic keynotes, interactive panel discussions, curated oral tracks, and comprehensive poster sessions tailored for deep exploration.

  • Direct Mentorship

    Benefit from dedicated one-on-one sessions and personalized guidance from seasoned domain experts, academic faculty, and industry veterans to accelerate your career.

How to Reach Plaksha University

By Air: Chandigarh International Airport (IXC) (~10 km / 15 mins). Pre-paid taxis, Uber, and Ola are available at arrivals.

By Train: Chandigarh Railway Stn. (CDG): ~15 km (25 mins), best for express and long-distance trains.

By Bus / Road: ISBT Sector 43, Chandigarh (~12 km) or ISBT Sector 17, Chandigarh (~13 km) or Mohali Phase 8 Stand (~8 km). Reach the campus via IT City Road using cabs, autos, or local CTU buses.
