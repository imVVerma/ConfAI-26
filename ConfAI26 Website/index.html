Conference Overview

The three day academic conference aims to highlight the potential of AI in exploring effective solutions to societal challenges. The talks and discussions aim to foster collaboration, knowledge sharing, and innovative discussions. It will bring together students, researchers, and practitioners across diverse AI domains to shape the future of research, development, and impactful applications in the Indian AI landscape.

Why Attend ConfAI 2026?

  • Prominent Keynote & Guest Speakers

    Engage with top leaders from industry and academia across 8 curated tracks.

  • Latest AI Research

    Attend cutting-edge research presentations highlighting the newest breakthroughs in artificial intelligence.

  • Practical Frameworks & Insights

    Learn frameworks, ideas, and explore critical questions around AI, innovation, emerging technologies, and their broader societal impact.

  • Collaborative Networking

    Build visibility for research collaborations and engage in high-impact industry conversations.
