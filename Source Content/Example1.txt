      <section class="event-overview" id="about" aria-labelledby="overview-title">
        <div class="section-shell overview-layout">
          <div class="overview-heading">
            <p class="eyebrow">About the event</p>
            <h2 id="overview-title">IJCAI Co-Located Event - Robot Expo at AICOR</h2>
          </div>
          <div class="overview-copy">
            <p class="overview-lead">
              The event offers IJCAI participants, researchers, and industry partners
              the opportunity to join a hands-on robotics exhibition to explore cutting-edge AI
              and robotics through scientific talks, live demonstrations, a panel discussion, and
              a networking reception. Hosted by the AICOR Institute for Artificial Intelligence at
              the University of Bremen, the event brings together the international AI research
              community and the German and European robotics ecosystem for an evening of
              scientific exchange and networking. The event will take place on 18 August 2026 from
              18:30 to 21:30.
            </p>
            <p class="institute-note">
              The AICOR Institute for Artificial Intelligence at the University of Bremen is a
              leading research institute dedicated to cognition-enabled robotics, advancing AI
              technologies that allow robots to autonomously understand and perform everyday tasks.
            </p>
          </div>
        </div>

                    <h4>Research demonstrations</h4>
                    <ul class="demonstration-list">
                      <li>Autonomous robots accomplishing everyday manipulation tasks</li>
                      <li>Laboratory robotics for scientific automation</li>
                      <li>Retail robots and service robotics in commercial settings</li>

                      <li>Robot perception and scene understanding</li>

                      <li>Plan-based control and AI-powered robot control architectures</li>

                      Booster Program.
                    </p>
